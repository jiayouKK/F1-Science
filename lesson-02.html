1. Unicellular vs Multicellular 单细胞 vs 多细胞

Unicellular 单细胞生物

Made of only 1 cell. That one cell does everything — feeding, moving, reproducing.

e.g. Amoeba, Paramecium, yeast

Multicellular 多细胞生物

Made of many cells. Different cells specialise in different jobs.

e.g. Human, cat, hibiscus
