考前速读版

1. Unicellular vs Multicellular

Unicellular

Made of only 1 cell. That one cell does everything — feeding, moving, reproducing.

e.g. Amoeba, Paramecium, yeast

Multicellular

Made of many cells. Different cells specialise in different jobs.

e.g. Human, cat, hibiscus

4. Specialised Cells 特化细胞

Different cells → different shapes → different jobs.

Animal cells
Nerve cell
Carries impulses around the body
Red blood cell
No nucleus; carries oxygen
White blood cell
Changes shape to destroy germs
Muscle cell
Contracts & relaxes for movement
Epithelium cell
Protects organs, secretes mucus
Sperm / Ovum
Carry genetic material for reproduction
Plant cells
Guard cell
Controls opening/closing of stoma
Palisade cell
Full of chloroplasts for photosynthesis
Epidermis cell
Reduces water loss, absorbs water
Root hair cell
Absorbs water & minerals

Stays orange-brown = no starch
