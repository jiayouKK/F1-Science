Chapter 3: Coordination and Response

考前速读版

1. What is Homeostasis?

你的身体其实内建了一个24 hour的aircon system，比你家的冷气还厉害——它不需要遥控器，自己会知道什么时候该开、该关。这套system就叫 Homeostasis。

Homeostasis = the body's ability to keep its internal environment constant (steady), no matter what's happening outside.

你身体里的温度、水分、糖分，一直被自动调节——不用你去想，它自己会处理。

2. Your Body's Built-in Thermostat

体温调节
🥵 Too hot
Sweat glands produce more sweat → evaporation cools the skin. Blood vessels near skin widen (vasodilation) → more heat lost.
🥶 Too cold
Shivering (muscles contract rapidly) generates heat. Blood vessels near skin narrow (vasoconstriction) → less heat lost.

生活化例子：夏天你会流汗降温；冬天冷到发抖，其实是肌肉在制造热量。

3. Water Regulation

水分调节
💧 Too little water
Feel thirsty → drink water. Kidneys produce less, more concentrated urine to save water.
💧 Too much water
Kidneys produce more, diluted urine to remove the excess.

生活化例子：喝很多水后，你会更常想上厕所——这就是身体在排掉多余的水分。

🎯 Quiz: When you're too hot, what happens to blood vessels near the skin?

4. Homeostasis in Plants — Transpiration

植物的体内平衡——蒸腾作用

Transpiration = loss of water vapour from a plant, mainly through tiny pores on leaves called stomata.

植物也有自己一套"调节系统"——透过控制气孔的开关，来平衡水分和气体交换。
