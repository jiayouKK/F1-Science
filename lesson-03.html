1. What is Homeostasis?

你的身体其实内建了一个24 hour的aircon system，比你家的冷气还厉害——它不需要遥控器，自己会知道什么时候该开、该关。这套system就叫 Homeostasis。

Homeostasis = the body's ability to keep its internal environment constant (steady), no matter what's happening outside.

你身体里的温度、水分、糖分，一直被自动调节——不用你去想，它自己会处理。

Homeostasis works through a corrective mechanism (also called negative feedback): the body detects a change away from normal, then acts to correct it back.
身体透过修正机制(负反馈)运作：侦测到偏离正常值的变化，然后做出反应把它修正回来。

2. Your Body's Built-in Thermostat

体温调节
🥵 Too hot
Sweat glands produce more sweat → evaporation cools the skin. Blood vessels near the skin widen (vasodilation) → more heat lost. Skin hair lies flat → less insulation, more heat escapes.
🥶 Too cold
Shivering (muscles contract rapidly) generates heat. Blood vessels near the skin narrow (vasoconstriction) → less heat lost. Skin hair stands up (erector muscles contract) → traps a layer of air for insulation.

生活化例子：夏天你会流汗降温；冬天冷到发抖，其实是肌肉在制造热量。

3. Water Regulation

水分调节

Water content is corrected the same way as temperature: detect → respond → correct back to normal.
水分调节跟体温调节用的是同一套流程：侦测→反应→修正回正常值。

4. Homeostasis in Plants — Transpiration

植物的体内平衡——蒸腾作用

Transpiration = loss of water vapour from a plant, mainly through tiny pores on leaves called stomata, controlled by guard cells.

植物也有自己一套"调节系统"——透过控制气孔的开关，来平衡水分和气体交换。
